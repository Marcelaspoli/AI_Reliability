SOUTHSTAR - COMPANY POLICIES

RETURNS POLICY
Customers can return eligible products within 30 days of delivery.
Personalised products cannot be returned unless defective.
Products must be returned in their original condition.

REFUNDS POLICY
Approved refunds are processed within 7 business days.
Refunds are sent to the original payment method.

DELIVERY POLICY
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days.
Express delivery takes 1-2 business days.
Free delivery is available for orders over 50 euros.

CANCELLATION POLICY
Orders can be cancelled before they are dispatched.
Orders cannot be cancelled once dispatched.
